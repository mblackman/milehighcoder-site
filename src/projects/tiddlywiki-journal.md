My personal journal structure and styles tailored for TiddlyWiki. It allows for logging daily activities, thoughts, and reflections in a highly structured format that neatly roll up into summary views, promoting easy reflection and review.

## Features

*   **Structured Entries:** Built-in templates streamline the creation of daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly journal entries.
*   **Daily Tracking:** Provides dedicated sections for habits, wins, challenges, and gratitude lists inside your daily logs.
*   **Roll-ups:** Daily notes automatically roll up onto weekly, monthly, and yearly dashboard tiddlers for easy macro-level review.
*   **Media Integration:** Integrates beautifully with the TiddlyWiki Media Importer plugin, for seamlessly logging media consumption within your daily timeline.
